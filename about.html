I’m Ivan

I didn't set out as a photographer. It started when I picked up a Fuji camera and began carrying it everywhere.

At first I just walked the streets and shot whatever caught my eye: people passing by, light falling across a sidewalk, small moments most people walk right past.

Over time I noticed I was drawn less to what was in the frame and more to how it was arranged. Lines, edges, shadows, the way a staircase cuts across a wall or a window repeats down a building. Color started to feel like a distraction, so I moved to black and white. These days that's most of what I look for.

This site is where I keep the ones I like. Thanks for stopping by and taking a look.
